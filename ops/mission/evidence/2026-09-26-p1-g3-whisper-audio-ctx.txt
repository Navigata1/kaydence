7. v3 SAFEGUARDS (operator decision 2026-09-27; this branch)             VERIFIED
   a) The fitted window is rounded up to a multiple of 8 (voxtype #130, "for GPU backend
      compatibility (Metal, Vulkan)"). The full window stays 1500.
   b) A degenerate fitted pass is re-run with the full 1500-frame window.
      whisper::degenerate_reason flags:
        - NoWords: ≥ 1 s of audio and no letter or digit (voxtype's rule);
        - RepeatedPhrase: one phrase ×3 back to back, ≥ 6 words (voxtype #124);
        - TooManyWords: > 6 words/s with ≥ 12 words.
      Unit fixtures:
        - #124 verbatim ("Please increase that limit 5x." ×3) → RepeatedPhrase;
        - the ADR-0024 sweep loop (the JFK sentence ×6 at 7.23 s) → RepeatedPhrase;
        - "", "...", "♪♪ ♪", "—" → NoWords;
        - 40 words in 2 s → TooManyWords;
        - real dictation, emphasis ("very very very", "no, no, no") and 5 words/s → none.
