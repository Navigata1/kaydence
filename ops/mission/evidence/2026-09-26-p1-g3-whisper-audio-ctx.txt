1. PROBLEM (measured on the unchanged adapter)                       VERIFIED
   reference-bench, 10 samples, 4.9 s JFK clip (sha256 5b0bcef3…):
     release→delivery-policy p50 3,606 ms · p95 4,062 ms (budget 1,200) FAIL
   asr_golden, 11.0 s JFK clip: transcribe 2,095 ms, RTF 0.190 → FAILED its
   1,200 ms assertion.
   Cause: every pass encodes a fixed 30 s window (~1.8 s per pass on this CPU
   regardless of speech length), and a new WhisperState is allocated per pass
   (14–167 ms measured).
