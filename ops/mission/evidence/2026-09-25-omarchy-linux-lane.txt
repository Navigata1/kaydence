     Found on the way (both recorded, harness-side fixed, product follow-ups):
     (a) FOCUS-BEFORE-TRACKING GAP (#8). First password run typed the dummy
         string into the throwaway password field: the window had focus
         BEFORE the AT-SPI tracker connected, so no focus-gain event reached
         it → Unknown → Lenient injects. The running app's tracker starts at
         launch, so ordinary use is covered (proved above), but a field
         focused before Kaydence starts — or a missed event — would be
         classified Unknown. Product fix (handoff K-23): at delivery, when no
         observation matches the focused pid, actively look up the focused
         accessible in that app (bounded walk, fail-safe on timeout).
         Harness now mirrors the app: focus leaves, tracker starts, focus
         returns.
     (b) GTK4 DROPS IN-FLIGHT KEYS ON FOCUS-OUT. With focus handed back right
         after typing, a GTK4 entry kept 16 of 28 characters (its input-
         method layer discards queued keys on leave); with a 600 ms settle,
         28/28. The selftest now settles before restoring focus. Product
         note: a user switching windows within ms of delivery could lose the
         tail in GTK4 apps — consider a short post-delivery settle.
